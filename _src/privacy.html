Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

1. Who is responsible

Besa Goods, Madrid, Spain, is responsible for the personal data processed on besagoods.com. Contact for all privacy questions: besasonos@gmail.com.

2. What we collect and why

  • Tea request form: name, email, country, tea, quantity, preferred language and your notes. We use them to source your tea, send you a price and shipping quote, and process your order if you accept. Legal basis: steps you ask us to take before a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)).
  • Contact form: email, subject and message, used only to reply to you (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).
  • Payment: if you decide to buy, you pay through a secure Stripe payment link. Stripe processes your card details; we never see them. We keep order and invoice records as tax law requires (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR).
  • Ad measurement (only if you accept cookies): Google Ads and, once enabled, the Meta pixel measure which ads bring visitors and requests. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Nothing from Google Ads or Meta loads before you accept.

3. Service providers

  • Formspree (Formspree, Inc., USA) delivers form submissions to our email.
  • Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe Ltd., Ireland) handles payments.
  • Google (Google Ireland Ltd.) for Google Ads, and Google Fonts, which loads the site's font from Google's servers and so receives your IP address.
  • Meta (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.) for the Meta pixel, only with your consent.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA) hosts the site and may log IP addresses for security.
  • Gmail (Google) is the mailbox where your requests and messages arrive.

Some providers are based in or transfer data to the USA. These transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

4. Amazon links

As an Amazon Associate, Besa Goods earns from qualifying purchases. When you click a “Shop on Amazon” link you go to Amazon, which processes your data under its own privacy notice. We only receive anonymous commission reports.

6. How long we keep data

Requests and messages that do not lead to an order are deleted within 12 months. Order and invoice data is kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

7. Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, restriction of processing, data portability, and you can object to processing or withdraw consent at any time. Email besasonos@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, for example the Spanish AEPD (aepd.es) or the authority where you live.

8. Changes

We will update this page if what we do with data changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
