Why cast iron

Brew it the way it deserves

Albanian mountain tea is brewed at lower temperatures than black tea and benefits from slow, even heat. Cast iron holds temperature longer than ceramic or glass — which means a better, smoother cup every time.

1

Heat the pot with boiling water first, then empty it before brewingNgroheni çajnikun me ujë të vluar fillimisht, pastaj zbrazeni

2

Add one heaped teaspoon per cup and brew for 5–7 minutesShtoni një lugë çaji të grumbulluar për filxhan dhe lini 5–7 minuta

3

Pour slowly. No milk. A small drop of honey if you likeHidheni ngadalë. Pa qumësht. Pak mjaltë nëse dëshironi
