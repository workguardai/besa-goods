What is Albanian Mountain Tea?

Albanian mountain tea, known as çaj mali or çaj i malit in Albanian, is a wild-harvested herbal tea from the Sideritis scardica plant. It grows naturally in the Albanian Alps above 1,200 metres. It is naturally caffeine-free, rich in antioxidants, and has been consumed in Albania for centuries. It has a floral, earthy taste with a refreshing aftertaste.
